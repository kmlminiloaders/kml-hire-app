How the estimate works

Estimated taxable business profit = recorded job income less expenses entered (adjusted by business-use percentage). The reserve is a planning estimate using the percentage in Settings. Your actual tax depends on your total personal taxable income, deductions, Medicare levy and other circumstances.

Because you also earn employment income, the app cannot accurately calculate your final personal tax from KML figures alone. Treat the reserve as money to put aside, not an ATO assessment.
